## 1. What we provide during the pilot

- An AI phone receptionist for your business, answering on a phone number we provide: [number]. Your own number stays yours; you choose when calls forward to ours (for example when you don't answer or are busy), and you can stop forwarding at any time.
- It answers questions using only the information you give us (services, prices, hours, policies and approved answers), books appointments into your calendar when slots are free, changes or cancels bookings when the caller confirms, and takes callback requests for anything else.
- A booking page for your customers and a staff dashboard for you, with email notices for new, changed and cancelled bookings and callback requests.
- Setup by us, using the details you give us in the intake.

The receptionist tells every caller it is an AI assistant. It never gives veterinary or medical advice; such questions become callback requests.

## 2. Price

The pilot is free. No card is needed, and nothing renews automatically. You pay for nothing unless you sign a separate paid agreement after the pilot.

## 3. What you agree to

- Give us accurate details about your services, prices, hours and policies, and tell us when they change.
- Use the service lawfully and only for your own business's calls and bookings.
- Check your dashboard or notice emails, and follow up on callback requests yourself. We take the messages; the conversation with your customer is yours.
- Tell us about anything the receptionist says or does that is wrong, so we can fix it.

## 4. Your customers' information

- The receptionist collects what a front desk would: callers' names, phone numbers or emails, their pets' names for pet businesses, the service and the appointment time. We use it only to run the receptionist for you.
- Calls are not recorded. Our phone provider processes the conversation live to understand and answer the caller, and may keep a text transcript for a limited time to operate its service.
- We use service providers to run the receptionist (hosting, phone numbers, speech and AI processing, email). They process information only to provide their part of the service. The current list is in our privacy policy at https://helloeverai.com/privacy/.
- We don't sell your customers' information or use it to market to them.
- Your booking data is yours. When the pilot ends without a paid agreement, we delete your business's data within 30 days of your request, or export it to you first if you ask.

## 5. No guarantees

This is a pilot of a new service. We work to keep it available and accurate, but we can't promise it will answer every call or never make a mistake (for example mishearing a name). Keep your usual way of taking bookings available, and check bookings in your calendar or dashboard. To the extent the law allows, neither of us is liable to the other for indirect losses, such as lost profits, during the free pilot.

## 6. Ending the pilot

Either of us can end the pilot at any time, for any reason, with an email. To stop calls reaching the receptionist straight away, turn off call forwarding on your phone. The pilot also ends on the end date unless we both agree in writing to continue.

## 7. General

- This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California.
- It is the whole agreement about the pilot. Changes need to be in writing (email is fine) and agreed by both of us.

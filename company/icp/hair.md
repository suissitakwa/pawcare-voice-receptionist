# Ideal customer: hair salons and barbers (after the shared core and Square)

Status: research leads now, but don't draft outreach until the hair package and Square connector are live.

## Who
- Independent hair salons and barbershops, **1–8 chairs**, US.

## Why they need us
- Stylists are mid-cut with their hands busy, and there's often no front desk.
- Service length varies a lot (cut vs. color vs. balayage), so booking needs care.
- Callers often ask for a specific stylist.

## Strong signals
- Uses **Square** or **Vagaro** (visible from the Book Now link), or no software.
- Reviews mention trouble reaching them or booking.
- No front-desk staff listed.

## Weak or no fit
- Chains (Great Clips, Supercuts, Sport Clips), walk-in only shops.
- Uses Fresha, Booksy or GlossGenius: possible, but only through the "text a booking link" fallback, so score lower.
- Large upmarket salons on Boulevard or Zenoti.

## Fit score
Same 1–5 scale as pet. Subtract 1 for Fresha/Booksy/GlossGenius.

## Pitch angle
"Your phone answered between every cut, booked into Square or Vagaro."

# Ideal customer: nail salons (later)

Status: research only. No outreach until pet and hair have paying customers.

## Questions to answer before we build
- What share of business is booked by phone versus walk-in?
- Which languages do owners and callers use (English, Vietnamese, Spanish, Korean...)? Does the receptionist need more than English?
- Which booking software do they use? GlossGenius and Booksy are common and have no open API.

## Who (working guess)
- Independent nail salons with 2–10 technicians and appointment-based services (gel, acrylic, nail art).

## Prospector task for now
Collect 20 example salons with booking software, appointment vs. walk-in signals and languages on the website. Mark them `status=new`, `fit_score` blank, and summarize patterns in `notes`.
